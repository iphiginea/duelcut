TMDB API Key

Duel Cut pulls posters and details from The Movie Database. Grab a free API key from themoviedb.org/settings/api and paste it below. It's stored only for you.

Backup & Sync

Export your rankings as a file, then import it in another browser or on another device to bring everything over. Importing replaces whatever's currently on this device.

Import from Letterboxd

In Letterboxd, go to Settings → Import & Export → Export Your Data, then upload the diary.csv or ratings.csv file from the downloaded zip. Each film gets matched on TMDB and seeded with a starting score based on your star rating.

Compare with a Friend

Have them export their own backup (Settings → Export Backup on their device) and send you the file. Upload it here to see how your tastes line up — this won't change your own rankings.
